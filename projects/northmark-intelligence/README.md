# Northmark Intelligence

A Toronto-based market research venture I'm building, using AI to produce Canadian commercial real estate research for private equity clients.

## Work so far

- **GTA CRE market brief.** Market-level research on the Greater Toronto Area across asset classes.
- **AI adoption strategy deck.** An 18-slide presentation for a private equity real estate firm on where AI fits in deal sourcing and asset management, with a data privacy framework. It doesn't push any one vendor.
- **Investor pitch.** A 9-slide deck on AI in real estate prepared for a Toronto real estate investment manager.
- **Guelph residential comp tracker.** Pulled sold low-rise listings into an Excel tracker with list-to-sale ratios, days on market, building age and links to each listing.
- **Muskoka Development Pipeline.** The first Northmark data product. See [the project page](../muskoka-development-pipeline).

## Product ideas tested

CAM reconciliation as a niche AI product, and a sustainability reporting platform. Neither moved past research.
