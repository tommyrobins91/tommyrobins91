# Industrial acquisitions underwriting

Anchor Capital Group, value-add industrial, Florida (2026)

## Scope

I've underwritten 50+ industrial deals across Plant City, Lakeland, Orlando, Tampa and Jacksonville, sourced from brokers and off-market. Deals range from small-bay multi-tenant buildings to a 155,000 SF single-tenant warehouse, plus sale-leasebacks, a multi-property portfolio, and a REO retail center.

All deals run on the firm's 39-tab acquisitions model. Deal-specific numbers are confidential and are not published here.

## What I've learned from the work

- **Entry price drives IRR more than anything else** on small-bay value-add. Rent growth assumptions rarely save a deal bought too high.
- **Negative leverage is common** at current rates, so many deals only work on a levered basis after rents reset.
- **In sale-leasebacks, lease term is the key variable.** A short term makes the seller's offered cap rate meaningless.
- **Contract assignment.** On one Orlando deal the investor bid didn't meet the target price, so I moved assignment outreach to owner-users, who value the building differently.

## Workflow I built around it

- Python and LibreOffice recalc scripts to find and repair cascading formula errors in large models
- Automated input documentation, so every assumption in a model is logged with its source
- Sale comps and property overview decks generated from data instead of built slide by slide
